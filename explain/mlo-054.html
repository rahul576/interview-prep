GPU utilisation across your training cluster is low and costs are high. How do you improve it?

MLOps · concept · levels senior, lead

In one line

Measure first with DCGM metrics per job, team and node, then fix with queueing and fair sharing (Kueue, Slurm) and quotas, right-sizing GPU types, sharing via MIG or time-slicing for small jobs and notebooks, idle detection and auto-shutdown, spot capacity with checkpointing, cost visibility per team, and faster input pipelines.

Detailed explanation

Low utilisation with high cost means GPUs are allocated but not used. The fix starts with data.

Measure. The NVIDIA DCGM exporter gives GPU utilisation, memory use, SM activity and power per GPU; joined with Kubernetes or Slurm labels, you get usage per job, team and node. Typical findings: interactive notebooks holding expensive GPUs idle for days; jobs requesting eight GPUs and using two; training jobs at 30 percent utilisation because data loading starves them; and GPUs reserved "just in case".

Queueing and quotas. Without a queue, teams grab and hoard GPUs. Kueue on Kubernetes (or Slurm, Volcano, Run:ai) admits jobs against team quotas with fair sharing, lets idle quota be borrowed by others, supports priorities and preemption, and gang-schedules distributed jobs. Jobs wait briefly instead of everyone reserving capacity permanently.

Right-sizing. Not every job needs an H100: small experiments, fine-tuning small models and notebooks can use smaller GPUs, MIG slices or time-sliced GPUs, while large distributed training gets full nodes with fast interconnects.

Idle detection. Shut down notebooks and dev endpoints after a period without GPU activity, with warnings; set TTLs on experiment clusters.

Spot capacity. Fault-tolerant training with regular checkpointing runs on spot or preemptible instances at a large discount.

Accountability and efficiency. Show cost and utilisation per team; it changes behaviour quickly. And fix the jobs themselves: faster data loaders (more workers, prefetching, local caching), mixed precision, larger batches, so running GPUs are busy.

How it works

  1. Deploy DCGM exporter; build dashboards by team, job and node.
  2. Identify idle allocations, oversized requests and starved jobs.
  3. Introduce queueing with quotas, borrowing and priorities.
  4. Offer MIG or time-sliced GPUs for small workloads.
  5. Auto-stop idle notebooks; move tolerant jobs to spot.
  6. Publish cost per team; help teams optimise data pipelines.

Example

apiVersion: kueue.x-k8s.io/v1beta1
kind: ClusterQueue
metadata: {name: team-search}
spec:
  cohort: ml-shared              # unused quota can be borrowed within the cohort
  namespaceSelector: {}
  resourceGroups:
  - coveredResources: ["nvidia.com/gpu"]
    flavors:
    - name: h100
      resources:
      - name: nvidia.com/gpu
        nominalQuota: 16
        borrowingLimit: 16
PromQL: avg by (namespace) (DCGM_FI_DEV_GPU_UTIL)  -> team-nlp notebooks: 3 %

Common mistakes

  • Buying more GPUs before measuring utilisation.
  • Static per-team reservations with no borrowing.
  • Notebooks allowed to hold full GPUs indefinitely.
  • Ignoring data-loading bottlenecks inside jobs.

How to answer in the interview

"First I measure GPU utilisation and memory per job and team from DCGM metrics. That usually shows idle notebooks holding GPUs, jobs requesting eight GPUs and using two, and data-loading bottlenecks. Then a queueing system like Kueue or Slurm with quotas and fair sharing, so jobs wait for capacity instead of hoarding, and idle quota can be borrowed. I right-size GPU types, give small jobs and notebooks MIG slices or time-sliced GPUs, auto-stop idle resources, run fault-tolerant training on spot with checkpointing, and show cost per team."

Likely follow-up questions

  • A team insists on reserved GPUs at all times? Give a guaranteed quota sized from their real usage, with borrowing and preemption for the rest, and show the cost.
  • Why is utilisation low in a running job? Data loading, small batches, CPU preprocessing or communication waits.
  • What metric matters besides utilisation? SM activity and memory bandwidth, since "utilisation" only means a kernel was running.
