How do you predict and prevent problems like disks filling up or certificates expiring?

AI Agents & AIOps · concept · levels entry, mid, senior

In one line

Predict exhaustion by forecasting usage trends and alerting on predicted time to full rather than current percentage, keep an inventory of certificates with expiry monitoring, and above all automate renewal and cleanup, with alerts timed to give enough lead time.

Detailed explanation

Disks filling up and certificates expiring are among the most predictable outages, yet they keep happening because alerts are poorly designed or the inventory is incomplete.

For disks, volumes, databases, queues and quotas, a percentage threshold is misleading. A disk at 70% growing 5% per hour will be full in six hours and is urgent; a disk at 92% that has been flat for months is not. Alerting on the predicted time until exhaustion captures what matters. In Prometheus, predict_linear() over a few hours of data gives a simple linear forecast; seasonal models handle daily patterns such as nightly batch jobs.

For certificates, the main problem is visibility. Keep an inventory of every certificate: load balancers, ingress controllers, internal mTLS, API gateways, code-signing and SAML signing keys, client certificates for partners. Monitor expiry with exporters (Blackbox exporter probes, cert-manager metrics) or cloud certificate services, alerting at around 30 and 7 days.

The real fix is automation: cert-manager with ACME or managed cloud certificates that renew automatically, log rotation and retention jobs, cleanup of old images and artifacts, and auto-growing storage where supported. Alerts then catch automation failures, timed so humans have enough lead time to act.

How it works

  1. Collect usage metrics over several hours to weeks.
  2. Forecast time to exhaustion per resource.
  3. Alert when predicted exhaustion is within the lead time needed to act.
  4. Discover and inventory certificates and their expiry dates.
  5. Automate renewal, rotation and cleanup.
  6. Alert on automation failure and near expiry.

Example

- alert: DiskWillFillIn4Hours
  expr: |
    predict_linear(node_filesystem_avail_bytes{fstype!~"tmpfs"}[6h], 4*3600) < 0
    and node_filesystem_avail_bytes / node_filesystem_size_bytes < 0.4
  for: 15m
- alert: CertificateExpiresSoon
  expr: (probe_ssl_earliest_cert_expiry - time()) / 86400 < 14
  labels: { severity: ticket }

With cert-manager, a Certificate resource renews automatically before expiry; the alert only fires if renewal is failing.

Common mistakes

  • Fixed 90% disk alerts that fire on stable volumes and miss fast growth.
  • Monitoring only public certificates and forgetting internal and signing ones.
  • Manual renewal processes tied to one person's calendar.
  • Alerts with too little lead time for procurement or approvals.

How to answer in the interview

"These are predictable failures, so forecasting prevents most of them. For disks and quotas I alert on predicted time to exhaustion, for example using predict_linear in Prometheus, instead of a fixed percentage: 70% growing fast is urgent, 90% and flat is not. For certificates I keep a full inventory, including internal and signing certificates, and monitor expiry at 30 and 7 days. The real fix is automation: cert-manager or managed certificates, log rotation and cleanup jobs, and auto-growing storage, with alerts mainly catching automation failures early enough to act."

Likely follow-up questions

  • Why is fixed 90% useless? It ignores growth rate and disk size.
  • Certificate discovery? Scan endpoints, cloud APIs and Kubernetes secrets.
  • Shorter certificate lifetimes? Public TLS lifetimes are shrinking, so manual renewal no longer scales.
