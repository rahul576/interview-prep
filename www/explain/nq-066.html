How would you calculate the total price of products in each category?

Coding Exercises · scenario · levels mid

In one line

groupingBy(Product::getCategory, summingDouble(Product::getPrice)) gives a Map<String, Double> of totals per category; alternatives are toMap with Double::sum as merge function, or reducing with BigDecimal; first confirm whether "total" means sum of prices or stock value (price × stock).

Detailed explanation

Clarify the requirement. "Total price per category" can mean the sum of unit prices (rarely meaningful in business) or the inventory value, price multiplied by stock. Asking this in an interview shows good judgement.

groupingBy with a downstream collector. Collectors.groupingBy(Product::getCategory, Collectors.summingDouble(Product::getPrice)) groups products by category and, instead of keeping lists, sums each group's prices on the fly. The result is Map<String, Double>. For inventory value, use a lambda: summingDouble(p -> p.getPrice() * p.getStock()).

toMap with a merge function. Collectors.toMap(Product::getCategory, Product::getPrice, Double::sum) maps each product to an entry; when two products share a category key, the merge function Double::sum adds their values. Without the merge function, duplicate keys throw IllegalStateException. A fourth argument supplies the map type, such as TreeMap::new.

Money with BigDecimal. Summing doubles accumulates rounding errors. With BigDecimal prices, use Collectors.reducing(BigDecimal.ZERO, Product::getPrice, BigDecimal::add) as the downstream, or toMap(..., BigDecimal::add). For inventory value: p -> p.getPrice().multiply(BigDecimal.valueOf(p.getStock())).

Several statistics at once. summarizingDouble(Product::getPrice) as downstream returns a DoubleSummaryStatistics per category with count, sum, min, max and average in a single pass.

Output ordering. The default map is a HashMap; use the map-factory overload with TreeMap::new for alphabetical categories when printing reports.

How it works

  1. Classify each product by category.
  2. For each group, a downstream collector accumulates the value.
  3. summingDouble adds doubles; reducing adds BigDecimals.
  4. The final map holds one total per category.

Example

Map<String, Double> totalPrice = products.stream()
        .collect(Collectors.groupingBy(Product::getCategory,
                 Collectors.summingDouble(Product::getPrice)));

Map<String, Double> stockValue = products.stream()
        .collect(Collectors.groupingBy(Product::getCategory, TreeMap::new,
                 Collectors.summingDouble(p -> p.getPrice() * p.getStock())));

Map<String, Double> viaToMap = products.stream()
        .collect(Collectors.toMap(Product::getCategory, Product::getPrice, Double::sum));

// BigDecimal prices
Map<String, BigDecimal> exact = items.stream()
        .collect(Collectors.groupingBy(Item::category,
                 Collectors.reducing(BigDecimal.ZERO, Item::price, BigDecimal::add)));

Map<String, DoubleSummaryStatistics> stats = products.stream()
        .collect(Collectors.groupingBy(Product::getCategory,
                 Collectors.summarizingDouble(Product::getPrice)));

Common mistakes

  • Not clarifying unit price sum vs stock value.
  • toMap without a merge function, failing on duplicate categories.
  • Summing doubles for money and printing results with rounding artefacts.
  • Grouping to lists first and summing each list in a second pass.

How to answer in the interview

"I'd write products.stream().collect(groupingBy(Product::getCategory, summingDouble(Product::getPrice))). groupingBy groups by category and the downstream summingDouble adds the prices in each group, giving a Map<String, Double>. An equivalent is toMap with Double::sum as the merge function for duplicate keys. I'd confirm whether total means the sum of unit prices or stock value, price times stock, which is just a different lambda. With BigDecimal prices I'd use reducing with BigDecimal.ZERO and BigDecimal::add."

Likely follow-up questions

  • Count, min, max and average in one pass? summarizingDouble as the downstream collector.
  • How do you sort the output by total? Stream the entry set and sort by Map.Entry.comparingByValue().
  • Why reducing for BigDecimal? There is no summingBigDecimal collector; reducing with add does it.
